# Online Estimation of Drainage Curves from Soil-Moisture Sensor Readings

Rita Almeida and Tiago Costa

## Abstract

We estimate the drainage curve of an irrigated plot from its own soil-moisture sensor readings, without laboratory calibration of the soil. After each irrigation event, the readings are fitted to an exponential decay towards field capacity, and the fitted rate is blended with the previous estimate so that one noisy event cannot move the curve far.

## 1 Introduction

Most smallholder farms schedule irrigation by habit rather than by measurement. Water is applied on fixed days and the amount depends on how long the pump runs.

## 4 Results

On laboratory-grade sensors, the online estimate converged within five irrigation events. We did not evaluate the method with missing readings or with sensor offsets.
