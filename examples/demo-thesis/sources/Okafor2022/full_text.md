# Capacitive Soil Sensors Under Field Conditions: Accuracy, Drift and Cost

Chidi Okafor, Ana Silva and Petr Novak

## Abstract

Capacitive sensors estimate soil water content from dielectric permittivity. Their readings depend on salinity, temperature and the contact between the probe and the soil. A complete sensor node now costs less than a day of hired farm labour in the regions we studied.

## 5 Field observations

After heavy rain, clay soils cracked around several probes and the readings showed sudden step changes that were not explained by the water applied.
